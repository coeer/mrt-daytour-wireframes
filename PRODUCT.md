# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS generated from one Python entry point and published through GitHub Pages.

## Users

The primary users are Korean-speaking travelers comparing a Sapporo-based Furano and Biei day tour on mobile. A Chinese version supports the merchant's review and content-maintenance workflow.

## Product Purpose

Help a traveler understand the route, pickup rules, included services, timing uncertainty, cancellation terms, and frequently asked questions well enough to decide whether the day tour fits their trip.

## Positioning

The offer combines pickup within 3 km of central Sapporo, operation from four guests, the Shikisai-no-Oka admission ticket, and a full refund when the group does not form. Drivers use Japanese or English and a translation app.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually review the page on a phone while planning a Hokkaido itinerary. They need to scan the day's sequence, compare travel effort with sightseeing time, and confirm pickup and cancellation details before booking through the selling platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three generated pages are required: Chinese, Korean, and a Korean-left/Chinese-right review page.
- Korean and Chinese product copy has one canonical source and must remain factually aligned across all outputs.
- All itinerary times are estimates and must say `예정` or `预计`.
- Cancellation is a full refund through three days before departure and no refund from two days before through the travel day; failed group formation receives a full refund.
- Drivers are described only as Japanese/English speaking with translation-app support.
- The page must not invent price, availability, ratings, reviews, inventory, urgency, certificates, or vehicle imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

- The approved v3 bilingual page and its generator contain the authoritative itinerary and FAQ copy.
- Licensed real destination photographs with verifiable source pages and attribution are available for Farm Tomita, Furano flower fields, Shikisai-no-Oka, Blue Pond, Shirahige Falls, and lavender soft serve.
- No verified price, rating, review count, inventory, or booking-availability data is available; future work must not fabricate it.

## Product Principles

- Product truth outranks promotional flourish.
- The route and timing should become understandable before decorative storytelling.
- Real location evidence should carry the page's persuasion.
- One content change must propagate consistently to all language outputs.
- Mobile reading is the primary experience, not a reduced desktop afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA text contrast, provide keyboard-visible focus, use semantic controls, keep targets at least 44 px high, respect reduced-motion preferences, and prevent Korean or Chinese text from clipping at supported breakpoints.
